# Human models

Sources verified 26 October 2026:
- Casual Man: https://grab3d.com/models/characters/man/
- Casual Woman: https://grab3d.com/models/characters/woman/

Both source pages explicitly license the models under CC0 1.0 (public domain).
Downloaded rigged GLBs: /dl/man-rigged/man-rigged.glb and /dl/woman-rigged/woman-rigged.glb.
Optimized locally with glTF Transform weld, simplify and resize (512px embedded textures).
Original embedded animation clips: walk and run. Additional gameplay poses are derived in code, not motion capture.
